# Salo: Product Requirements Document (V2)

**Product Vision:** Salo is not a budget tracker; it is a "Household Operating System" with a heart. It sits between cold financial apps (Splitwise) and unstructured chat apps (Messenger). Its core value is providing *legibility without surveillance*, reducing financial guilt, and acting as a scrapbook of family care for OFWs and their families.

## 1. Core Principles (The "Anti-Budget Tracker")
- **Warm Vocabulary:** No "Debt," "Balance," or "Analytics." We use "Padala," "Budget sa Bahay," "Needs," and "Mga Naitawid."
- **Achievements > Pie Charts:** Expenses are framed as family wins (e.g., "Na-Cover Na!") rather than budget drains.
- **Asynchronous Peace:** No read receipts or typing indicators. It is a sacred, quiet space away from the noise of group chats.

## 2. Key Features (Phase 2)

### A. Countdown to "Uwi" (The Emotional Anchor)
- **Description:** A persistent, shared date at the top of the feed showing exactly how many days are left until the OFW comes home.
- **Why:** Grounds all the financial sacrifice in the ultimate goal—reuniting the family.

### B. The "Covered!" Celebration Loop (Need → Expense linking)
- **Description:** Families post a `Need` (e.g., "Pambili ng gamot ni Lola"). When the OFW sends money or the family buys it, they log an `Expense` and link it to the Need. The original Need gets a beautiful "🎉 Covered!" badge.
- **Why:** Gives the OFW the "Provider's High." It clearly closes the loop on requests, removing the awkwardness of asking for money and wondering if it was received.

### C. "Padala" Celebrations (Remittance Logs)
- **Description:** A new specific post type for when the OFW sends money. Accompanied by a festive, rewarding animation (e.g., confetti).
- **Why:** Normalizes and celebrates the act of remitting money, automatically updating the "Budget sa Bahay" context.

### D. Weekly Recap bilang "Kwento" (Narrative Reports)
- **Description:** Instead of a dashboard chart, the app auto-generates a warm sentence at the end of the week. E.g., *"Ngayong linggo: 3 grocery run, pambayad sa tuition ni Ana, at 12 hearts na pinalitan 💛"*
- **Why:** Translates raw financial data into a human story.

## 3. Future Roadmap (Phase 3+)
- **"Sabi Mo" Voice Notes:** Audio-clip attachments for posts so OFWs can hear their family's voice.
- **The "Balikbayan Box" Wishlist:** A digital box where kids can drop wishes, and the OFW can check them off over months.

## 4. User Journeys

### Journey 1: The Request & Fulfillment (Family → OFW)
1. Misis posts a Need: "Kailangan ng pambayad sa kuryente bukas" (Amount: ₱2500).
2. OFW sees the Need asynchronously on their break in Dubai. OFW reacts with a "Kaya natin 'to" custom reaction.
3. OFW sends the Padala.
4. Misis pays the bill, logs the Expense, uploads the receipt, and links it to the Need.
5. The Need is stamped with "Covered!". OFW feels accomplished.

### Journey 2: The Weekly Catch-up (OFW)
1. OFW opens the app on a Sunday.
2. Sees the Countdown to Uwi (e.g., "140 days na lang!").
3. Reads the Weekly Kwento summarizing the family's activities and expenses.
4. Scrolls through the feed to heart/comment on specific receipt photos.

## 5. Success Metrics
- **Emotional ROI:** Reduced frequency of arguments/anxiety about money via user feedback.
- **Engagement:** High ratio of Reactions per Post (proving the app is being used for connection, not just logging).
- **Completion Rate:** Percentage of `Needs` that successfully transition to `Covered!`.
